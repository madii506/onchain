01 · How it works

Two signatures. One transaction.

No escrow, no middleman key, no pool. Your coins never leave your wallet until the moment they swap.

  1. 01

    Make an offer

    Pick any coin and any wallet's bag, then set your price in SOL. Your wallet signs your half of the swap in advance. The SOL stays with you.

    advance nonce → your SOL → their wallet
  2. 02

    The holder signs

    They see it on their desk, or you send them the link. One signature accepts it. You can cancel at any moment before they do.

    their tokens → your wallet
  3. 03

    One transaction swaps both

    Tokens go one way and SOL goes the other in a single Solana transaction. If either side is short, nothing moves.

    all or nothing · 0.00% chart impact

07 · Questions

How the swap stays safe

Who holds my coins while an offer is open?

You do. Your offer is a transaction your wallet signed in advance, waiting for the holder's signature. Your SOL and their tokens stay in each wallet until the swap. onchain holds no key that can move anything.

What is the deposit slot?

Each open offer rests on a small Solana "durable nonce" account. It lets your signed half stay valid for days instead of about a minute. It holds about 0.001 SOL of rent, which goes back to your wallet when you cancel or reclaim it. Only your wallet controls it.

How do I cancel?

Click cancel on your desk. Your wallet closes the deposit slot, which makes your signed half invalid forever, and the rent returns to you.

Why does it not move the chart?

The tokens and SOL go straight from wallet to wallet in one transaction. Nothing is sold into the curve or the pool, so the price never sees it.

What if the holder sold first, or I spent my SOL?

Then the swap fails as a whole and nothing moves. Both transfers happen together or not at all.

Which coins work?

Any SPL or Token-2022 coin on Solana, pump.fun or not. Coins with a transfer hook are not supported yet.

How does the holder find my offer?

It shows on their desk the next time they connect, and you get a link to send them. Anyone can check the offer on its page.
